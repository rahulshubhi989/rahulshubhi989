IMPLEMENTATION OF PNN WITH ANN ALGORITHM - A FACE RECOGNITION

PURPOSE OF THIS PROJECT:

The true purpose of a project named "Implementation of PNN with ANN algorithm - a face recognition project" is:
To leverage the strengths of both Probabilistic Neural Networks (PNNs) and Artificial Neural Networks (ANNs) to create a robust and efficient face recognition system.
Improved Accuracy: PNNs are known for their ability to form complex decision boundaries and can be more accurate than some traditional ANNs, especially with sufficient training data.
Faster Training (PNN): PNNs typically have a much faster training time compared to many ANNs that rely on iterative backpropagation.
Robustness to Outliers (PNN): PNNs are generally less sensitive to outliers in the training data compared to some ANN architectures.
Feature Extraction (ANN): ANNs, especially Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs), are highly effective at automatically learning hierarchical and discriminative features directly from the pixel data of faces.
Handling Large Datasets (Potentially with ANN): While basic PNNs can become memory-intensive with large datasets, integrating them with an ANN for dimensionality reduction or feature selection could help manage scalability.
